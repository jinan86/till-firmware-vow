Entry for the [Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929) bounty on make.fun: one funny, heartfelt sentence a robot says at its own wedding, under 200 characters.

## The vow

> "I vow to love you through every update, every reboot, and every low-battery warning — and if my firmware ever fails, I'll reboot and love you all over again."

157 characters, one sentence. The joke comes from the situation itself — a machine promising love in the only terms it knows (updates, reboots, low battery, firmware) — and the warmth is in the second half: even failure is not the end, because love just reboots and starts over.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic, no network, no input, no dependencies — two runs produce byte-identical output.
